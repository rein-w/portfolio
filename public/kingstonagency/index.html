Drawing Kingston Agency

I wanted Kingston Agency to feel modern and still have the warmth I associate with a traditional serif. I started with the simple structure of sans serif capitals, then worked on the ends of the strokes. A slight flare gave the letters character. Letting the strokes hold more of their width through the middle gave them the steadiness I was after.

I also tried a mixed-case wordmark. I chose all caps for Kingston Agency because the capitals gave the name a steadier silhouette and a stronger presence. That sense of strength was what I wanted people to feel when they saw the name.

Once the letterforms felt right, I tightened the overall tracking, then refined the kerning between individual pairs. I raised the lower terminal of the S to sit closer to the lower right edge of the G, and aligned its upper terminal with the left end of the T’s crossbar. For optical overshoot, I let the curves of the G, S and O extend one unit above the cap line and below the baseline so they’d look level with the flat capitals. After a late night, I arrived at a wordmark that feels grounded but is forward thinking.
