Design challenge

  • The warmth of a traditional serif in a modern, all-caps wordmark.
  • A slight serif flare at the stroke ends and a subtle taper through the stems.
  • Added stroke weight for a steadier silhouette.
  • Gently rounded corners to soften the capitals.

I also tried a mixed-case wordmark. I chose all caps for Kingston Agency because the capitals gave the name a steadier silhouette and a stronger presence. That sense of strength was what I wanted people to feel when they saw the name. Once the letterforms felt right, I tightened the overall tracking, then refined the kerning between individual pairs.

I raised the lower terminal of the S to sit closer to the lower right edge of the G, and aligned its upper terminal with the left end of the T’s crossbar. For optical overshoot, I let the curves of the G, S and O extend one unit above the cap line and below the baseline so they’d look level with the flat capitals. After a late night, I arrived at a wordmark that feels grounded but is forward thinking.
